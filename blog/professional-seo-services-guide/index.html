What Are SEO Services?

SEO services are professional offerings from freelancers or agencies that help businesses show up higher in Google and other search engines. These services usually include content creation, link acquisition, technical fixes, and ongoing monitoring.

From my experience working with over 50 clients in the past 4 years, I have seen that businesses who invest in proper SEO services see real results in 4 to 6 months. One of my clients in the SaaS space came to me with zero organic traffic. After three months of consistent on-page optimization and technical fixes, their traffic grew to over 2000 monthly visitors.

Types of SEO Services

SEO is not one size fits all. Based on what I have learned from 50+ client projects, here are the main categories of SEO services that actually deliver results.

On-Page SEO Services

On-page SEO is the foundation. Without it, ranking well on Google is nearly impossible. I focus on picking the right keywords and creating content that answers user questions.

What I typically do for on-page SEO:

  • Find keywords that your customers actually search for (not just high volume ones)
  • Fix title tags, meta descriptions, and heading structure
  • Improve existing content to make it more helpful and unique
  • Add internal links to related pages
  • Ongoing monitoring and updates as search trends change

Link Building Services

Link building is about getting other websites to link back to your content. Search engines treat backlinks like recommendations. The more quality sites link to you, the more trust your site earns.

My approach to link building includes:

  • Guest posting on relevant sites in your industry
  • Finding broken links and offering your content as a replacement
  • Building real relationships with site owners and editors (no spam)
  • Creating useful resources that people want to link to naturally

In one of my recent projects, a single guest post on a DR 60+ publication brought over 300 referral visits and helped a key page move to page 1.

Technical SEO Services

Technical SEO ensures search engines can properly crawl and understand your site. If Google cannot access your content easily, you will not rank well.

Key technical areas I focus on:

  • Improving page speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Fixing crawl errors and indexing issues
  • Setting up XML sitemaps and robots.txt correctly
  • Adding structured data for rich snippets

I worked with an ecommerce site that was loading in 5 seconds. After compressing images and cleaning up the code, load time dropped to under 2 seconds. Organic traffic increased by 25% within two months.

Local SEO Services

Local SEO helps service-based businesses show up when people search for things like "plumber near me" or "best bakery in Lahore".

What local SEO includes:

  • Setting up and optimizing Google Business Profile
  • Building local citations to keep NAP consistent
  • Managing customer reviews and responding to them
  • Creating locally focused content

A dental clinic I worked with was not showing up in local search at all. After optimizing their GBP and getting genuine reviews, they started appearing in the Local Pack and received 50+ calls in the first month.

Ecommerce SEO Services

Ecommerce SEO applies to online stores. It focuses on product pages, category pages, and shopping feeds.

Typical ecommerce SEO work:

  • Optimizing product titles and descriptions
  • Fixing duplicate content issues
  • Adding product schema markup
  • Managing Google Merchant Center feed

An online fitness gear store had 50+ product pages not ranking. After rewriting product descriptions and fixing internal links, organic sales increased by 35% in 3 months.

AI Search Optimization GEO

Search is changing. More people now ask ChatGPT and Perplexity instead of Google. Optimizing for AI search is becoming important.

What I do for AI search optimization:

  • Write content that AI models can easily understand
  • Use clear headings and structured lists
  • Build brand mentions that AI can cite as sources
  • Include facts, data, and specific examples

Red Flags to Avoid When Hiring SEO Help

  • Guaranteed rankings. No one controls Google. Anyone who promises a #1 spot is either lying or using risky black hat tactics.
  • "Secret sauce" language. If they cannot explain their process clearly, they are probably not doing real work.
  • Vanity metric reports. Be careful of providers who only talk about "impressions" or "keyword counts" instead of leads and sales.
  • Promises of quick results. SEO takes time. Anyone promising overnight rankings is likely using automation that can get your site penalized.

Once you start working with SEO services, you will need to track your progress. Using rank tracking tools helps you see what is actually working and where you need to improve.
