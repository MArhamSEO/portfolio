Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to show results?
Domain Rating (DR) can increase within 2-3 months through quality backlinks and guest posting. However, significant ranking improvements and organic traffic growth typically take 4-6 months.
Do you use black hat SEO techniques?
No, absolutely not. I strictly follow white-hat SEO practices recommended by Google. I do not use PBNs, link farms, keyword stuffing, or any other black hat techniques.
How does Local SEO work for my business?
Local SEO helps your business appear in Google's Local Pack and Maps results. I optimize your Google Business Profile, manage NAP consistency across directories, build local citations, and track metrics like calls and CTR.
What social media platforms do you specialize in?
For organic growth (SMO), I specialize in Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok. For paid ads (SMM), I focus on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok.
Do you offer free revisions?
Yes, two rounds of revisions are included free of charge for every project. Additional revisions may incur extra charges depending on the scope.
Can you sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?
Absolutely. I respect client confidentiality and am happy to sign an NDA before starting any project.
What kind of reporting do you provide?
I provide detailed monthly reports including keyword rankings, organic traffic analysis, backlink profiles, conversion data, and ROI analysis.
Do you take urgent or rush orders?
Yes, depending on my current workload. Rush orders may incur an additional fee depending on the urgency and complexity.
