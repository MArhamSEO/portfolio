Services I Offer

Search Engine Optimization

I provide complete SEO solutions to help businesses rank higher on Google. Services include website audit, on-page optimization (meta tags, headings, keywords), technical SEO (site speed, crawlability), mobile-friendly optimization, schema markup, content optimization, and Core Web Vitals improvement. I have helped 50+ clients achieve top 3 rankings and significant organic growth across various industries including SaaS, healthcare, e-commerce, and technology.

Link Building and Guest Posting

Link building is essential for increasing website authority and search rankings. I specialize in guest posting on websites with DA 50+ and strong Domain Rating (DR), HARO link building for authoritative backlinks from major publications, broken link building, competitor backlink analysis, niche edits, and local link building.

Industries I specialize in:

  • Health and Medical: Dental clinics, medical practices, healthcare blogs, wellness websites, telemedicine platforms.
  • SaaS: B2B SaaS companies, tech startups, software review platforms, cloud computing services.
  • Technology and IT: Tech blogs, IT services, cybersecurity websites.
  • E-commerce: Online stores, product review websites, retail blogs.
  • Cryptocurrency and Blockchain: Crypto news sites, Bitcoin blogs, Web3 platforms.

I have successfully published over 500 guest posts on high-quality, relevant websites that drive real referral traffic and improve search engine rankings. All placements are on sites with good domain rating (DR 50+) and genuine organic traffic.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

I help businesses dominate local search results and appear in Google's Local Pack. Services include Google Business Profile optimization, NAP consistency across Yellow Pages, Justdial, Yelp, review management, local citations, proximity versus authority optimization, and tracking local metrics including CTR, calls, direction requests, dwell time, branded searches, and engagement. I specialize in helping local businesses rank number one in their city.

Social Media Optimization

Grow your brand organically without paid ads. Services include profile optimization, organic reach growth, engagement rate improvement, branding consistency, content strategy, audience psychology, retention tactics, and organic growth strategies including hashtag strategy and collaborations. I specialize in growing brands on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok.

Social Media Marketing

Generate leads and sales through paid ads. Services include paid ads strategy, lead generation, sales funnel management, retargeting, conversion tracking and ROAS optimization, audience targeting including lookalike and custom audiences, A/B testing, and scaling winning campaigns. I have managed over $50,000 in ad spend with average 3x to 5x ROAS.

Ads Campaign Management

I build complete campaign architectures on Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube) and Meta Ads Manager. Services include campaign structure, audience targeting, A/B testing, CTR optimization, bid strategy, retargeting, conversion tracking via Google Tag Manager and Meta Pixel, ad fatigue management, and scaling strategies that maintain cost per acquisition and return on ad spend.
