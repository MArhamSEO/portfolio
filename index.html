Services I Offer

🔍 Search Engine Optimization (On-Page & Technical)

I provide complete SEO solutions to help businesses rank higher on Google. My services include website audit, on-page optimization (meta tags, headings, keywords, internal linking), technical SEO (site speed, crawlability, indexation), mobile-friendly optimization, schema markup, content optimization, and Core Web Vitals improvement. I've helped 50+ clients achieve top 3 rankings and significant organic traffic growth.

🔗 Off-Page SEO & Link Building

Link building is key to increasing website authority. I specialize in guest posting on DA 50+ sites, profile backlinks, HARO link building (Forbes, Entrepreneur), broken link building, competitor backlink analysis, niche edits, and local link building. I've successfully published 500+ guest posts and helped clients build strong, natural backlink profiles that Google loves.

📍 Local SEO & Google Business Profile

For businesses targeting local customers, Local SEO is essential. I help businesses dominate local search results and appear in Google's Local Pack (the top 3 results on Google Maps). My Local SEO services include complete Google Business Profile optimization including profile setup and verification, selection of primary and secondary categories that best represent your business, optimization of your services section with detailed descriptions, uploading geo-tagged images to build trust, managing Q&A section by answering customer questions professionally, posting weekly updates on GBP (offers, events, news, products), ensuring NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone number) across all online directories like Yellow Pages, Justdial, Yelp, managing review velocity and responding to customer reviews, understanding and optimizing for proximity vs authority (Google's local ranking factors), and tracking local metrics such as CTR, calls, direction requests, dwell time, branded searches, and engagement. I help local businesses rank #1 in their city and dominate their local market.

Social Media Optimization helps grow your brand organically without paid ads. I help businesses build a strong social media presence that attracts, engages, and retains followers.
