Services I Offer

Search Engine Optimization

I provide complete SEO solutions to help businesses rank higher on Google. Services include website audit, on-page optimization (meta tags, headings, keywords), technical SEO (site speed, crawlability), mobile-friendly optimization, schema markup, content optimization, and Core Web Vitals improvement. I focus on sustainable white-hat SEO strategies that deliver long-term results.

Link Building and Guest Posting

Link building is essential for increasing website authority. I specialize in guest posting on websites with DA 50+ and strong Domain Rating (DR), HARO link building for authoritative backlinks from major publications, broken link building, competitor backlink analysis, niche edits, and local link building.

Industries I specialize in: Health, SaaS, Technology, E-commerce, Crypto. I have successfully published over 500 guest posts on high-quality, relevant websites that drive real referral traffic and improve search engine rankings. All placements are on sites with good domain rating (DR 50+) and genuine organic traffic. I do not use black hat techniques or PBNs.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

For businesses targeting local customers, Local SEO is essential. I help businesses dominate local search results and appear in Google's Local Pack (the top 3 results on Google Maps). My Local SEO services include complete Google Business Profile optimization including profile setup and verification, selection of primary and secondary categories that best represent your business, optimization of your services section with detailed descriptions, uploading geo-tagged images to build trust, managing Q&A section by answering customer questions professionally, posting weekly updates on GBP, ensuring NAP consistency across all online directories like Yellow Pages, Justdial, Yelp, managing review velocity and responding to customer reviews, understanding and optimizing for proximity vs authority, and tracking local metrics such as CTR, calls, direction requests, dwell time, branded searches, and engagement. I help local businesses rank #1 in their city and dominate their local market.

Social Media Optimization

Social Media Optimization helps grow your brand organically without paid ads. I help businesses build a strong social media presence that attracts, engages, and retains followers. Services include complete profile optimization (bio, profile images, cover photos, links, username optimization), organic reach growth using algorithm-friendly content strategies, improving engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, saves, mentions), building a consistent brand identity (visual elements, tone of voice, brand colors), developing a content strategy with 3-5 main content pillars that align with your brand, understanding audience psychology to create content that resonates with your target audience, implementing retention and loyalty tactics to keep followers engaged over time, and using organic growth tactics such as hashtag strategy, trending audio, collaborations, and user-generated content. I specialize in growing brands on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok. My focus is on building an authentic community around your brand that leads to long-term customer loyalty.

Social Media Marketing

Social Media Marketing (paid ads) is the fastest way to generate leads, sales, and brand awareness. I create and manage high-performing ad campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok. My SMM services include developing a paid ads strategy tailored to your business goals, running lead generation campaigns to capture quality leads at the lowest possible cost per lead (CPL), sales funnel management (top-of-funnel awareness, middle-of-funnel consideration, bottom-of-funnel conversion), retargeting campaigns to convert warm audiences who have already shown interest but haven't purchased yet, conversion tracking and ROAS optimization using Facebook Pixel, Google Tag Manager, and event tracking, advanced audience targeting including lookalike audiences (1%, 2%, 3% lookalikes based on your best customers), custom audiences (website visitors, email lists, video viewers), and interest-based targeting, A/B testing of ad copy, images, videos, headlines, CTAs, and landing pages to find winning combinations, and scaling winning campaigns with proper bid strategies without losing profitability.

Ads Campaign Management

As a professional Ads Campaign Manager, I build complete campaign architectures that drive measurable results. I specialize in Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube, Performance Max) and Meta Ads Manager (Facebook & Instagram). My services include campaign structure setup with proper hierarchy of campaigns, ad groups, ads, and keywords for maximum organization and performance, advanced audience targeting using in-market segments, affinity audiences, custom segments, detailed demographics, and lookalike audiences to reach the right people at the right time, A/B testing of headlines, descriptions, CTAs, display paths, and landing pages to continuously improve performance, CTR optimization through compelling ad copy and creative design that stops the scroll and drives clicks, CPC control and bid strategy management to ensure you're never overpaying for clicks, retargeting campaign setup for both website visitors and engaged social media users to bring back warm traffic that didn't convert the first time, conversion tracking implementation via Google Tag Manager, Facebook Pixel, and Google Analytics 4 to accurately measure every conversion and attribute it to the right campaign, ad fatigue management by regularly refreshing creatives to prevent audience burnout, and scaling strategies that increase budget profitably while maintaining or improving CPA and ROAS.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to show results?
Domain Rating (DR) can increase within 2-3 months through quality backlinks and guest posting. However, significant ranking improvements and organic traffic growth typically take 4-6 months. I focus on sustainable white-hat SEO strategies that deliver long-term results, not quick shortcuts that risk Google penalties.
Do you use black hat SEO techniques?
No, absolutely not. I strictly follow white-hat SEO practices recommended by Google. I do not use PBNs, link farms, keyword stuffing, or any other black hat techniques. All my strategies are sustainable and risk-free.
How does Local SEO work for my business?
Local SEO helps your business appear in Google's Local Pack and Maps results. I optimize your Google Business Profile, manage NAP consistency across directories like Yellow Pages and Justdial, build local citations, and track metrics like calls, direction requests, and CTR to improve local visibility.
What social media platforms do you specialize in?
For organic growth (SMO), I specialize in Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok. For paid ads (SMM), I focus on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok. Each platform strategy is tailored to your specific audience and business goals.
Do you offer free revisions?
Yes, two rounds of revisions are included free of charge for every project. Additional revisions may incur extra charges depending on the scope of changes requested.
Can you sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?
Absolutely. I respect client confidentiality and am happy to sign an NDA before starting any project. Client data and project details are never shared without permission.
What kind of reporting do you provide?
I provide detailed monthly reports including keyword rankings, organic traffic analysis, backlink profiles, conversion data, and ROI analysis. All reports are customized based on your specific goals and KPIs.
Do you take urgent or rush orders?
Yes, depending on my current workload. Rush orders may incur an additional fee of 25-50% depending on the urgency and complexity of the project.
