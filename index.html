Services I Offer

🔍 Search Engine Optimization

I provide complete SEO solutions to help businesses rank higher on Google. Services include website audit, on-page optimization (meta tags, headings, keywords), technical SEO (site speed, crawlability), mobile-friendly optimization, schema markup, content optimization, and Core Web Vitals improvement. I've helped 50+ clients achieve top 3 rankings and significant organic growth.

🔗 Link Building & Guest Posting

I specialize in guest posting on DA 50+ sites, HARO link building, broken link building, competitor backlink analysis, niche edits, and local link building. I've successfully published 500+ guest posts and helped clients build strong, natural backlink profiles that Google loves.

📍 Local SEO & Google Business Profile

I help businesses dominate local search results and appear in Google's Local Pack. Services include GBP optimization, NAP consistency across Yellow Pages, Justdial, Yelp, review management, local citations, proximity vs authority optimization, and tracking local metrics (CTR, calls, direction requests).

📱 Social Media Optimization (SMO)

Grow your brand organically without paid ads. Services include profile optimization, organic reach growth, engagement rate improvement, branding consistency, content strategy, audience psychology, retention tactics, and organic growth strategies (hashtags, collaborations).

💰 Social Media Marketing (SMM)

Generate leads and sales through paid ads. Services include paid ads strategy, lead generation, sales funnel management, retargeting, conversion tracking & ROAS optimization, audience targeting (lookalike, custom), A/B testing, and scaling winning campaigns. Managed over $50,000+ ad spend with 3x-5x ROAS.

📊 Ads Campaign Management

I build complete campaign architectures on Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager. Services include campaign structure, audience targeting, A/B testing, CTR optimization, bid strategy, retargeting, conversion tracking via GTM & Pixel, ad fatigue management, and scaling strategies that maintain CPA and ROAS.
