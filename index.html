Services I Offer

Search Engine Optimization

I provide complete SEO solutions to help businesses rank higher on Google. My services include comprehensive website audit, on-page optimization (meta tags, headings, keyword optimization, internal linking, URL structure), technical SEO (site speed optimization, crawlability, indexation, XML sitemaps, robots.txt), mobile-friendly optimization, Core Web Vitals improvement (LCP, FID, CLS), schema markup implementation for rich snippets, content optimization with keyword mapping and topic clusters, and site structure improvement with breadcrumbs and silo structure. I focus on sustainable white-hat SEO strategies that deliver long-term results without risking Google penalties.

Link Building and Guest Posting

Link building is essential for increasing website authority and search rankings. I specialize in guest posting on DA 50+ websites with strong Domain Rating (DR), HARO (Help a Reporter Out) link building to earn backlinks from Forbes, Entrepreneur, and other major publications, broken link building, competitor backlink analysis, niche edits, profile backlinks creation, and local link building. I have successfully published over 500 guest posts across multiple industries including SaaS, Health and Medical, Technology and IT, General Business, E-commerce, and Cryptocurrency / Blockchain / Web3. All placements are on high-quality, relevant sites with genuine organic traffic. I do not use black hat techniques or PBNs.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Local SEO helps businesses appear in Google's Local Pack and Maps results. I optimize your Google Business Profile (GBP) with primary and secondary category selection, services optimization, geo-tagged images, business description, and product/service sections. I manage Q&A section, post weekly updates, ensure NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone) across Yellow Pages, Justdial, Yelp, and other directories. I handle review velocity and response management, build local citations, and optimize for proximity vs authority. I track local metrics including CTR, calls, direction requests, dwell time, branded searches, and engagement to improve local visibility.

Social Media Optimization

Social Media Optimization helps grow your brand organically without paid ads. I optimize your social media profiles (bio, images, links, username), increase organic reach through algorithm-friendly content strategies, improve engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, saves), build consistent brand identity and tone of voice, develop content strategy with 3-5 main content pillars, understand audience psychology for better engagement, implement retention and loyalty tactics, and use organic growth strategies including hashtag research, trending audio, collaborations, and user-generated content. I specialize in Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok.

Social Media Marketing

Social Media Marketing is the fastest way to generate leads, sales, and brand awareness. I create and manage high-performing ad campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok. My services include paid ads strategy, lead generation campaigns, sales funnel management (top, middle, bottom of funnel), retargeting campaigns to convert warm audiences, conversion tracking and ROAS optimization using Facebook Pixel and Google Tag Manager, advanced audience targeting (lookalike audiences at 1%, 2%, 3%, custom audiences based on website visitors and email lists, interest-based targeting), A/B testing of ad copy, images, videos, headlines, and CTAs, and scaling winning campaigns with proper bid strategies without losing profitability.

Ads Campaign Management

As a professional Ads Campaign Manager, I build complete campaign architectures on Google Ads (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube, Performance Max) and Meta Ads Manager (Facebook & Instagram). Services include campaign structure setup with proper hierarchy of campaigns, ad groups, ads, and keywords, advanced audience targeting using in-market segments, affinity audiences, custom segments, and lookalike audiences, A/B testing of headlines, descriptions, CTAs, display paths, and landing pages, CTR optimization through compelling ad copy and creative design, CPC control and bid strategy management (maximize clicks, target CPA, maximize conversions, target ROAS), retargeting campaign setup for website visitors and engaged users, conversion tracking via Google Tag Manager and Facebook Pixel, ad fatigue management by refreshing creatives, and scaling strategies that increase budget profitably while maintaining CPA and ROAS.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take to show results?
Domain Rating (DR) can increase within 2-3 months through quality backlinks and guest posting. However, significant ranking improvements and organic traffic growth typically take 4-6 months. I focus on sustainable white-hat SEO strategies that deliver long-term results, not quick shortcuts that risk Google penalties.
Do you use black hat SEO techniques?
No, absolutely not. I strictly follow white-hat SEO practices recommended by Google. I do not use PBNs, link farms, keyword stuffing, or any other black hat techniques. All my strategies are sustainable and risk-free.
How does Local SEO work for my business?
Local SEO helps your business appear in Google's Local Pack and Maps results. I optimize your Google Business Profile, manage NAP consistency across directories like Yellow Pages and Justdial, build local citations, and track metrics like calls, direction requests, and CTR to improve local visibility.
What social media platforms do you specialize in?
For organic growth (SMO), I specialize in Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok. For paid ads (SMM), I focus on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok. Each platform strategy is tailored to your specific audience and business goals.
Do you offer free revisions?
Yes, two rounds of revisions are included free of charge for every project. Additional revisions may incur extra charges depending on the scope of changes requested.
Can you sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?
Absolutely. I respect client confidentiality and am happy to sign an NDA before starting any project. Client data and project details are never shared without permission.
What kind of reporting do you provide?
I provide detailed monthly reports including keyword rankings, organic traffic analysis, backlink profiles, conversion data, and ROI analysis. All reports are customized based on your specific goals and KPIs.
Do you take urgent or rush orders?
Yes, depending on my current workload. Rush orders may incur an additional fee of 25-50% depending on the urgency and complexity of the project.
